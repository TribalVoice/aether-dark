Part of the Aether instruments. Original dual-voice drone — not affiliated with any hardware manufacturer. Use headphones. Click POWER to enable audio. MIDI: notes set oscillator pitch, pitch-bend ±2 st, CC1 LFO rate, CC7 master, CC71/74 resonance/cutoff I, CC76/77 resonance/cutoff II, CC91 bloom, CC94 echo. Web MIDI needs a secure context (https or localhost). On iPad use Safari, turn volume up, tap POWER, then use the on-screen keys. Recording to tape may be unavailable in Safari. Free to use. Support: ko-fi.com/tribalvoice
